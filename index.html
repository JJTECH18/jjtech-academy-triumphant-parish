About this compound

Two callings, raised side by side

JJTECH Academy and RCCG Triumphant Assembly Parish share more than a street address. One shapes minds through the week; the other shapes character through worship — and together they form a single community that watches its children grow in both.

This site is the shared front door to both. Pick a gate above, or use the menu at any time to move between the Academy and the Parish — term dates, service times, admissions and ministries all live one click away.
